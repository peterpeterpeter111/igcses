# Design checkpoint - 9 September 2026

Commits `0ce2cfd`, `97a1314` and `2cf46e7` rework the visual direction without changing the content, routes or completion ledger.

- The home page now presents subjects as horizontal-name books on a dark, crimson bookshelf.
- Each subject page has a black lacquer-style book panel on the left for course information.
- Chapters remain in ascending order on a smooth, side-curving vertical SVG path; selecting a stop expands a book-page panel and keeps the existing chapter link.
- Oni labels and crests were removed. The open subject panel and chapter stops now use flower-petal accents only.
- Subject entries are individual standing book shapes on the shelf rather than flat rows.
- The chapter route has a muted crimson base and a progressive dull-gold overlay. Selecting a chapter lights the route from the beginning through that chapter, while tree markers frame the route.
- Motion remains CSS-only and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Validation: TypeScript, production build and the existing 32-test suite passed during this design phase. No curriculum, paper, template, AI or deployment counts changed.

The local preview can be reviewed at `http://localhost:3000/` and any subject route. GitHub and Sites publication remain separate pending the next available write window and final product verification.
