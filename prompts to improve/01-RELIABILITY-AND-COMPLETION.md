## 1. Prove what the resource covers

Build a student-readable specification explorer for all six exact qualification codes and issues. ## 2. Make explanations educationally sufficient

For every required concept, provide an accurate definition, causal explanation or derivation, a concrete example, a contrasting misconception, a worked application and a transfer question. Science units, significant figures, diagrams, variables and practical limits must be checked. Maths needs valid notation, domains, equivalent forms, working and suitable constructions. English needs appropriate source-text ranges, whole-response exemplars, level-grid judgement and explicit separation of reasonable interpretations from unsupported claims.

Have qualified independent reviewers audit representative material from every chapter. Record findings, repair substantive issues, then review the repair. Do not label self-generated synthetic tests as independent examiner calibration.

## 3. Validate assessment instead of trusting fluent AI

Activate only versioned question families with source-linked demand, independently checked generated solutions, unambiguous marking rules and held-out response evidence. Audit every 3-, 4- and 6-mark adaptation as its own demand; do not pad a two-mark task into six marks. Keep all schemes server-side until the full attempt is submitted.

Build a held-out benchmark of permitted, de-identified responses marked independently by trained examiners. Include correct, partially correct, contradictory, terse, long but irrelevant, arithmetic-error, alternative-method, multilingual and adversarial responses. Measure exact mark agreement, adjacent agreement, criterion-level false awards/denials, severe misgrades and variation across repeated calls. Pre-register acceptance thresholds with reviewer justification, report confidence intervals and subject/mark-band slices, and withhold underperforming families. Never fabricate a pass rate.

Check the requested five exact free OpenRouter model identities, modalities, routing, context limits and account access. Evaluate the actual team against simpler alternatives using the same benchmark. Keep the requested roster unless the owner explicitly approves a replacement. Preserve unresolved marking disagreements, the original proposals and evidence. AI explanations must cite the learner's actual answer and its frozen rubric, not a guessed scheme.

## 4. Definition of done

Deliver the reconciled ledger, review reports, measured benchmark results, repaired defects, regression tests, responsive/accessibility checks and exact deployed source/version.
