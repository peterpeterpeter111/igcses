# Implementation prompt: demonstrate value worth paying for

Start by verifying the latest coverage and assessment gates. Do not add billing or make commercial claims before the educational product works reliably. Do not implement these proposals without a later instruction to do so.

Research students', parents' and teachers' actual revision problems through consented interviews and task-based trials. Record evidence and compare current alternatives using current primary sources. Prioritise a small set of demonstrated benefits: complete accurate teaching, dependable question practice, transparent marking, useful error repair and clear revision decisions. Do not invent testimonials, learning gains or willingness-to-pay results.

Run a bounded pilot across all six subjects with baseline and follow-up transfer/retention tasks. Separate engagement from learning outcomes and report sample sizes and limitations. Use independently marked responses to audit feedback quality. Resolve severe educational errors before recruiting more learners.

Prepare operational reliability: realistic free-provider quotas, latency and outage behaviour; a resumable review queue; per-browser/account and global abuse budgets; retry limits; provider status; clear service availability; and accessible support/correction reporting. Verify whether free endpoints can serve the intended audience. Do not promise unlimited free AI or silently switch to a paid provider. A reviewed fallback requires explicit owner approval and its own educational evaluation.

Prepare a sustainable cost model using measured requests, tokens, Composio charges where applicable, hosting/storage, support and reviewer time. Obtain owner approval for all paid services or subscriptions. Propose pricing only after value and costs are evidenced. Keep pricing honest about included usage, outages, renewal and cancellation; do not implement dark patterns or guaranteed-grade claims.

Before charging, review content permissions, privacy/retention, minors' needs, security, backups/recovery, account boundaries, deletion/export and applicable consumer obligations with suitable professional input where needed. Verify mobile performance, keyboard/screen-reader access and reduced motion. Conduct a real restore rehearsal and a private-data isolation test.

Deliver a launch recommendation with explicit go/no-go gates, measured evidence, unresolved risks, ongoing review responsibilities and a correction/service incident process. A polished interface alone is not a commercial-readiness pass.
